KSLBestDungeon Changelog
========================

V1.5

New:

- Your keystone is marked in the ranking: the dungeon of the key in your bags
  shows a keystone icon with its level under the rank, and the row is
  lightly highlighted, so you can see at a glance whether it is a good loot
  key.
- Each dungeon shows your season best under its score ("Best +12 | 285
  rating", or "Not done this season"), so you can weigh loot against rating
  when choosing a key. The row tooltip has both too.
- Both are for the character you are logged in on, so they show only when
  KeystoneLoot is showing that character, not one of your alts.
- Your group's keystones show too. In a group, opening the Best Dungeons
  tab asks everyone for their key, and any dungeon someone holds a key for
  is marked in blue with the highest level, and listed by name in the row
  tooltip - handy for deciding whose key to run. Groupmates need an addon
  that shares keys the usual way (BigWigs, Details, EllesmereUI and many
  others do); KSLBestDungeon itself needs nothing extra for this.
