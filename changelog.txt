KSLBestDungeon Changelog
========================

V1.6

- Group Keys: opening Blizzard's Create Group form for a dungeon now shows
  a small Group Keys panel beside the Group Finder window (next to
  Raider.IO's profile panel when that is showing), listing your
  keystone and every groupmate's key that their addons share (BigWigs,
  Details, EllesmereUI and others that speak LibKeystone). Click one and
  the form switches to that dungeon, with its key level in the title, ready
  to list - no more hunting through the dropdown for a groupmate's key.
  Listing a key you do not hold needs the Blizzard Authenticator on your
  account; that is Blizzard's own rule, and the form says so.
